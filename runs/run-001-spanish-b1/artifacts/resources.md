# Resources — Conversational European Spanish for Travel

## Summary
The **video** curator variant ran for this pipeline (frontmatter `owner` is the pipeline
slot `curator`, per contract). Nine modules are covered with 2–4 resources each, 26
resources total, of which 20 are video (76.9%, clearing gate G8's 70% floor). The backbone
providers are **LightSpeed Spanish** (a Spain-based, English-taught YouTube channel/podcast
— the single strongest fit for Peninsular pronunciation, vosotros and ser/estar) and
**Language Transfer's** free "Complete Spanish" audio course (used sparingly, as audio, for
grammar induction that LightSpeed doesn't cover cleanly at beginner tier — the preterite
"dot in the past" and "voy a" lessons). **Dreaming Spanish** (filtered to Spain-accent
hosts) supplies comprehensible-input listening practice for modules 5, 6 and 9. **BBC's
Mi Vida Loca** (a Madrid-set beginner video drama) fills survival, directions and
cumulative-listening slots, though its official `bbc.co.uk/languages` site was abandoned
in 2014 — it survives only through third-party YouTube reuploads, which is flagged below.
Two modules (6 and 8) needed a non-video or partial-region resource to stay honest rather
than force a weak video citation; both are flagged in Open Questions. Cost position: every
cited resource is free (YouTube, SoundCloud, and the providers' own free blog pages), so
the run spends **EUR 0 of the EUR 50 budget** — comfortably inside the "strongly favour
free" instruction. No two modules share a URL (verified below); every citation carries a
`verified:` marker dated 2026-08-18 from this run.

## Open Questions
- **BBC Mi Vida Loca's official home is gone.** `bbc.co.uk/languages` was abandoned by the
  BBC around 2014 (confirmed via websearch of Association for Language Learning coverage);
  the content now survives only through third-party YouTube reuploads (English commentary,
  Spanish subtitles). Authority for the *content* is still BBC (a recognised institution),
  but the *distribution* is unofficial and could disappear without notice. I judged this an
  acceptable risk given the material's quality and Peninsular setting, but the coordinator
  should know it is not an evergreen link the way an active institutional page would be.
- **Two resources are not video, and one is region-mixed, by necessity, not oversight:**
  module 6's comparatives resource (SpanishDict) is text because no Peninsular-specific
  comparatives *video* cleared the Authority bar in the time available; module 8's Rick
  Steves video covers "Spain and Latin America" together rather than Peninsular-exclusive,
  because no free, Authority-passing, Spain-only video on service/problem exchanges was
  found. Both are flagged rather than silently substituted. The overall video ratio
  (76.9%) still clears gate G8.
- **SpanishPod101 has an explicitly labeled "Absolute Beginner European Spanish" series**
  (confirmed via webfetch — lesson 55, "10 Phrases to Help You in an Emergency," names
  Spain-specific detail) that would have been an excellent fit for module 8. It is excluded
  because its free tier covers only the first 3 lessons of each series and our target
  lesson is #55 — the free tier does not cover what would be assigned, which fails this
  run's free-budget instruction even though the paid tiers ($4–47/month) would technically
  fit the EUR 50 ceiling. Recorded here in case the learner later decides to spend part of
  the budget on it.
- **Module 8 is the weakest-covered module.** Neither resource is a clean Peninsular-only,
  video, service/emergency-specific lesson. If the coordinator wants a stronger match here,
  the SpanishPod101 lesson above (paid) or a targeted follow-up search for a Spain-based
  creator's "reporting a problem" video would be the next things to try.
- **Speaking practice is intentionally out of scope for this artifact.** None of the cited
  resources are interactive — LightSpeed, BBC and Dreaming Spanish are all receptive
  (listening/watching) even where they model spoken dialogue. Per `requirements.md`, target
  outcomes 1–3 require dedicated speaking practice that a video resource cannot itself
  provide; `exercise-designer` is expected to build the role-play, shadowing and
  self-recording exercises the curriculum's modules 2–9 all name as spoken-production
  objectives.
- **Duration fields marked `unknown`** are individual LightSpeed YouTube videos whose exact
  runtime I could not confirm without opening individual watch pages, which this run was
  instructed to avoid due to consent-wall loops in a prior attempt. Playlist- and
  platform-level totals are estimates based on confirmed per-episode lengths where
  available (e.g., the 22×~10 min Mi Vida Loca structure) and are marked "(est.)".
- **Captions:** Dreaming Spanish confirms English/Spanish subtitles across its library; the
  BBC Mi Vida Loca reuploads explicitly advertise "English Commentary, Spanish Subtitles";
  LightSpeed's YouTube videos carry YouTube's standard auto-captions (not individually
  verified per video). Language Transfer is audio-only; a volunteer-made transcript PDF for
  the Complete Spanish course exists online, which partially offsets the lack of captions
  for a non-native reader, though it was not verified as officially maintained.
